Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and handled when you use our Facebook Page Management Application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate through Facebook Login, we may access certain information authorized by you via the Meta Graph API:

  • Profile Information: Your Facebook User ID and public profile name.
  • Facebook Pages Data: Page IDs, page names, categories, and Page Access Tokens for pages you manage.
  • Content & Insights: Posts, media, publication status, and page analytics (reach, impressions, reactions, and views).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your account and list your Facebook Pages.
  • Publish and schedule posts, photos, carousels, reels, and stories to your pages.
  • Track engagement metrics and send audience milestone notifications.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data or Facebook content with third parties or advertisers. All operations are strictly executed through the official Meta Graph API.

4. Data Storage and Security

All access tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored locally in our private database with restricted access.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Facebook Platform rules, you can request the deletion of your data or revoke app permissions at any time:

  • Via Facebook: Go to your Facebook account settings at https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools, select this application, and click Remove to revoke access and request data deletion.
  • Via Direct Email: You can also send an email request to delete all stored tokens and page data to dubrinmining@gmail.com. We will process your request and delete all records within 48 hours.

6. Contact Us

For any questions or privacy inquiries, please contact us at: dubrinmining@gmail.com
